# PRODIGY_CS_07_Keylogger

A keylogger (short for keystroke logger) is a surveillance tool, either software or hardware, that secretly 
records every keystroke made on a computer or mobile device. It captures sensitive data like 
passwords, credit card numbers, and messages. Synonyms include keystroke logger, keyboard 
capturing, and spyware.  

Key Aspects: 
• Malicious Usage: Hackers use keyloggers for credential theft, identity theft, and stealing 

corporate data. 
• Legitimate Usage: Employers or parents may use them for employee monitoring or parental 

supervision on devices they own. 
• Types: Software keyloggers are malicious applications, while hardware keyloggers are physical 

devices attached to the keyboard or USB port. 
• Functionality: Beyond keystrokes, they can capture screenshots, clipboard content, and 

camera or microphone activity. 
• Cybercrime: Stealing login credentials for banking or social media websites. 
• Corporate Surveillance: Monitoring employee productivity or protecting against intellectual 

property theft. 
• Parental Monitoring: Supervising children’s online activities.
